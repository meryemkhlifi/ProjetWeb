Our Mission

At Odin, we believe that every person deserves dignity, independence, and quality care, especially when facing the challenges of Alzheimer's disease. Our mission is to empower both patients and caregivers with technology that simplifies care management while preserving autonomy.

We're committed to:

  • Enhancing Quality of Life
  • Supporting Independence
  • Connecting Communities
  • Advancing Research

Every feature we develop is guided by our core belief that technology should serve humanity with compassion, especially during life's most challenging chapters.

Privacy

Security First Approach

Your data security is our top priority. Odin employs end-to-end encryption for all sensitive information, ensuring that personal and medical data remains protected at every stage. Our systems are regularly audited by independent security experts to maintain the highest standards of protection.

Your Data, Your Control

We believe you should have complete ownership of your information. Odin provides transparent data management tools that allow you to view, export, or delete your data at any time. We never sell your personal information to third parties, and data sharing for research purposes is strictly opt-in with clear consent processes.

Respecting Patient Rights

We recognize the unique privacy considerations for individuals with Alzheimer's. Odin is designed to balance necessary caregiver oversight with appropriate privacy and dignity for patients. Our privacy settings can be customized to evolve with changing care needs, always prioritizing respect for the individual while ensuring safety.

For complete details on how we protect your data, please review our full Privacy Policy.
